## Task

Track the weight of backlinks for link builders and SEOs — see which links hold their weight and which have lost it over time.

## What came before

A set of Python scripts, each for its own piece of the process. The first ran through a list from Excel, the second parsed search results, the third checked link indexing, the fourth collected the result into a new Excel file, the fifth built reports and dashboards.

Now one service does the same thing — instead of five-plus scripts run by hand one after another.

## How it's built

I wrote it from scratch myself, in Claude Code, with no development team.

## What worked

The service is running, monetized and has active clients.

## What didn't work

The AI features laid out in the roadmap are not shipped. The reason isn't technical — they don't pay for themselves.

The API subscriptions they need cost more than 200 dollars a month in total, and the project's net profit for a month is less than that. While the cash gap holds, the features wait.
