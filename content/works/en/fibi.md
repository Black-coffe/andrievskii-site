## Task

A financial marketplace where a user can compare and choose financial products.

## What came before

A few small sites of a couple of pages each, built for financial arbitrage — mostly for microloans in Ukraine.

Now it is one portal, and no longer only about microloans: banks, insurance, crypto. Larger in scope and in three languages.

## How it's built

Three developers write it, each in Claude Code on their own machine, kept in sync and working together on one codebase.

## What worked

A working product with a distributed team, where every developer uses Claude Code as their main tool.

## What didn't work

A fully self-sufficient content machine didn't come out. The idea was this: AI providers and search-results parsing providers update the old content and write new content themselves, with no human involved.

For the text to be good and accepted by search engines, 20–25% still goes to manual review by copywriters and SEO specialists. A hundred percent automation didn't work out.
