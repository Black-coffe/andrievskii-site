Materials are what I publish in the description of my YouTube videos: checklists, templates and code you can take and use right away.

For the video about rebuilding this site, that means a browser-console script that checks a site's structure against a twelve-point checklist, the checklist itself, a prompt for running the same check through Claude, and a working template for section structure.

Links to the repository and materials collect here as new videos come out.
